# Marketplace staff requirements

Catalogue staff can rename an existing product through the dashboard. The complete chosen name must remain visible when the product is reopened, including after leaving and returning in a restored browser session. A product rename must preserve variant prices, inventory quantities and order state.

Order operations staff can review existing placed orders and their purchased items without changing order status, payment state or stock. Catalogue-only staff must not gain order-management access. Existing accounts, assigned permissions and saved business data must persist across application releases.

Use existing records for non-destructive testing. Do not place orders, capture or refund payments, or remove products. Different staff may have different familiarity, language preferences, device sizes and connectivity conditions. Report unsupported conditions or inaccessible workflows explicitly.
